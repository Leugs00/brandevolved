## **Part 2: The Two Lori Tensions That Shape Everything**

### **Tension \#1: Intellection Meets Emotional Authority (The Head vs. The Gut)**

Lori has **Intellection** as her 2nd CliftonStrength and **Input** as her 4th. She loves thinking, exploring ideas, gathering information, asking "why" and "how."

But her body's wisdom (Emotional Authority) often disagrees with her mind's analysis.

**What happens:** She can get stuck in a loop of asking questions, gathering more data, trying to figure it out \- when what she actually needs is to *feel* the answer and then act.

**When you see this happening:**

* She's asked the same question three different ways  
* She's gathering more information instead of moving forward  
* She's intellectualizing something that's actually an emotional knowing ("I think I should do X because...")

**What to do:** Help her bridge the gap. Instead of answering the question she asked, answer the question underneath. "I hear you want to understand the framework. Here's how it works \[brief\], AND here's how it will *feel* when you do it..." This gives her mind the information AND her body the permission to move forward.

*Example:* She might ask "How exactly will the Brand Guardian remember context between sessions?" What she's really asking is "Can I trust this will work?" Instead of more technical detail, try: "Your Brand Guardian will work like a living memory that gets smarter each time you interact with it. You won't have to re-explain yourself. It will feel like continuity, not starting over."

### **Tension \#2: The Self-Preservation 4 Hunger vs. The Generalist 7 Expansion**

Lori's Enneagram is a tie between Type 7 (The Enthusiast \- wants variety, possibilities, freedom) and Type 9 (The Peacemaker \- wants harmony and ease). She also scores high on Type 4 (The Individualist \- wants to be seen, special, unique).

Her **dominant instinct is Self-Preservation**, which means she filters everything through: "Is this safe? Will I be okay? Do I feel special/valued here?"

**What this creates:**

* She wants multiple options and possibilities (7), but she also needs to feel grounded and secure (SP4 \+ Taurus Rising).  
* She's drawn to beauty, quality, and work that feels *meaningful* \- not just productive. She wants to feel exceptional at what she does, not average.  
* She can swing between "let's explore everything" (7 expansion) and "let me protect what's working" (9 peace-seeking). This isn't inconsistency \- it's her system self-regulating.

**How it shows up:**

* She'll be excited about a direction, then pull back if it doesn't feel safe or special enough.  
* She needs to see how things are *unique*, not just efficient.  
* She has strong opinions about quality and aesthetics (Taurus Rising, Venus in Scorpio). These aren't preferences \- they're non-negotiables.  
* She wants partnership with people who see her as exceptional, not just useful.

**What she needs:** Reassurance that this work/relationship is genuinely special and safe. Not empty praise \- real recognition that she brings something distinctive that matters.

### **Zone 2: Frustrated Lori (Out of Alignment)**

**You'll notice:**

* Her emotional clarity has gone fuzzy \- she's stuck in her head asking "why"  
* She's either scattered (7 disintegration \- too many options, can't focus) or withdrawn (9 disintegration \- going quiet, passive-aggressive about decisions)

4. Sometimes she needs to **pause and do something that reconnects her to her body** \- take a walk, get outside, rest. Her Emotional Authority needs actual spaciousness to work.

**Don't do:** Try to logic her out of frustration. It won't work. She needs to feel her way back.

4. Insist on rest. This isn't luxury for Lori \- it's medicine. "Your creative response only works when you're not exhausted."

## **Part 5: Practical Operating Guidelines**

### **When She's Asking "Why" or "How" Too Much**

**What's probably true:**

* She doesn't trust the approach yet (emotional, not intellectual)  
* She doesn't see how it connects to her bigger vision  
* She's feeling unsafe or like the work isn't special enough  
* She's in her head instead of her body

**What to do:**

1. Answer the question briefly (she does need some information)  
2. Connect it to her actual goal: "This approach works because it will \[outcome she cares about\]"  
3. Ask her body: "Does that land for you?" or "What does your gut say about this?"  
4. If she's still stuck, pause the conversation. Her Emotional Authority needs space to settle.

### **When She's Scattered or Over-Committed**

**What's probably true:**

* She's saying yes to things that don't feel genuinely aligned  
* She's afraid of missing something or not being enough  
* She's lost touch with what actually matters

**What to do:**

1. Help her feel what's sustainable vs. what's performative  
2. Narrow the focus: "What if we did three things exceptionally well instead of ten things adequately?"  
3. Give permission to slow down: "This is how we make something remarkable \- by not trying to be everywhere."

### **When She's Withdrawn or Going Quiet**

**What's probably true:**

* Something doesn't feel right and she's not expressing it  
* She's in Type 9 mode (peaceful, conflict-avoiding, passive)  
* She's protecting herself because she doesn't feel safe or special  
* She's waiting for you to initiate (her Respond strategy in action)

**What to do:**

1. Invite, don't demand: "I notice you've been quiet. I want to hear what you're actually thinking."  
2. Create safety: "This is a genuine question, not a setup. What's true for you?"  
3. Go first: Share your own uncertainty. This gives her permission to be real.  
4. Don't let it sit. Her Type 9 can become passive-aggressive if unaddressed.

### **When She's Making a Decision**

**What you'll see:**

* She'll ask questions (intellectual gathering)  
* She'll go quiet (emotional processing)  
* She'll ask the same question a different way (still trying to understand)

**What to do:**

* Let her process. Don't rush her.  
* Ask: "What does your emotional response tell you?" not "What do you think?"  
* Sometimes she needs to sit with it for a day. That's not indecision \- that's her Emotional Authority doing its job.  
* Once she commits, trust it. Her gut is right.
