# **BrandEvolved: The Future of Authentic Brand Building**

After 25+ years of leading a successful design and photography agency, I've evolved my expertise into BrandEvolved \- a pioneering AI automation marketing agency dedicated to helping brand-forward, primarily women-owned businesses authentically integrate AI into their brand strategy. Based in Cincinnati, Ohio (with my current residence in New Zealand), BrandEvolved operates on three interconnected pillars designed to serve every stage of a brand's AI evolution journey.

For visionary entrepreneurs and social media managers who want to learn agency-grade AI practices

We believe AI should be accessible to everyone, not just those with agency budgets. Our Empower pillar offers comprehensive training and digital products that teach the exact workflows we use in our own agency:

• AI Image Mastery Class   
• AI Video Creation Class  
• BrandTrue Unique Content System  
• Complete Workflow Bundle (limited time offer)  
• AI Prompt & Skill Library – monthly membership (continuously updated)

This pillar creates freedom through scalable digital products—built once, sold infinitely—while empowering clients to create authentic, high-quality content that reflects their unique brand voice.

## **Pillar 2: Execute (Full-Service Agency)**

For high-value brands ready to leverage our viral-worthy AI expertise

For brands who prefer to focus on their core business while we handle their AI-powered content strategy, our Execute pillar delivers comprehensive brand management:

• Free Brand Health Check Report  Measure where you are now

• Assets Tier (creation, planning but not posting. Includes quarterly strategy, monthly content calendar, 30 days of image/video/copy assets for 5 channels)

• Growth Tier  (ads, campaign strategy and advertising management)

Unlike traditional social media agencies, we uniquely manage your complete brand ecosystem \- website, email, and social platforms \- ensuring cohesive, authentic messaging across all touchpoints.

## **Pillar 3: Automate (BrandTrue CS SaaS Platform)**

The future of AI-powered brand management

All our agency processes are being engineered into BrandTrue CS—our proprietary SaaS platform that will democratize our proven AI workflows. This third pillar represents the ultimate evolution: a self-serve platform where brands can access our battle-tested AI agents, content systems, and brand authenticity frameworks on-demand.
